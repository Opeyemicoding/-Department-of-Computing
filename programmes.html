Our Programmes

The Department of Computing offers programmes designed to develop students' computing knowledge and practical skills.

National Diploma (ND)

This programme introduces students to computer science, programming, computer systems and information technology.

Higher National Diploma (HND)

This programme develops advanced knowledge and practical skills in software development, databases, networking and other computing areas.
